它没有剧本It has no script

你看到的每一步路、每一次洗脸、每一次起飞,都不是程序员写的「如果…就…」。是 45,808 个真苍蝇的神经细胞,在你的手机里一秒钟放电一万步,投票投出来的。

Every step it takes, every face-wash, every take-off — none of it is an "if this, then that" written by a programmer. It is 45,808 nerve cells from a real fly, stepping ten thousand times a second inside your phone, voting.

这张「大脑地图」是真的The brain map is real

科学家把一只真果蝇的脑子,切成了几千片超级薄的薄片 (每片比头发丝还薄得多),用电子显微镜一张一张拍下来, 再把每个细胞的形状描出来,数清楚谁连着谁。

Scientists took the brain of one real fruit fly and cut it into thousands of incredibly thin slices — each far thinner than a hair — photographed every slice under an electron microscope, then traced each cell and counted who connects to whom.

这个工程叫 FlyWire,全世界的科学家和志愿者 (其中不少是普通玩家!)一起干了好几年,才画完这张图:

一个神经细胞 = 一个会漏水的小杯子One nerve cell = one leaky cup

神经细胞不会思考。它只会一件事:攒信号,攒够了就「啪」一下放电。

A nerve cell does not think. It does exactly one thing: it collects signal, and when there's enough — snap — it fires.

  • 💧 别的细胞放电,就往它的杯子里倒一点水
  • 🕳️ 杯底有个小洞,水一直在慢慢漏掉——所以要倒得够快才攒得起来
  • ⚡ 水位碰到红线 → 放电!然后杯子倒空,重新攒
  • 🥄 还有些连接不是倒水,是舀水(叫「抑制」),专门劝它闭嘴
  • 💧 When another cell fires, it pours a little water into this one's cup
  • 🕳️ The cup has a small hole — water keeps leaking out, so the pouring has to be fast enough
  • ⚡ Water reaches the red line → it fires! — then the cup empties and starts over
  • 🥄 Some connections don't pour, they scoop water out ("inhibition") — their whole job is saying shush

科学家管这个叫「漏水的积分放电」模型(leaky integrate-and-fire)。你脑子里也是这么工作的——只不过你有 860 亿个杯子。

Scientists call this the leaky integrate-and-fire model. Your brain works the same way — you just have 86 billion cups.

老实说:哪些是真的,哪些是我们做的Honestly: what's real, and what's ours

✔ 真的✔ Real

  • 每个细胞什么时候放电(论文里的规则)
  • 它的每一个「决定」:吃不吃、跳不跳、往哪转
  • The 45,808 cells and every connection between them
  • When each cell fires (the published rules)
  • Every "decision": to eat or not, to jump or not, where to turn

✎ 我们做的✎ Ours

  • 3D 草地、镜头、光——它的「玩具世界」
  • 把「放电快慢」翻译成腿和翅膀的动作
  • 一个很小的「出去逛逛」推力(不然它会一直发呆)
  • 「Fed 饱不饱」那条,是我们帮它记的账
  • The 3D meadow, the camera, the light — its toy world
  • Translating firing rates into leg and wing motion
  • One tiny "go wander" nudge (or it would daydream forever)
  • The "Fed" bar — bookkeeping we do on its behalf
一句话:大脑是真的,身体和世界是我们给这颗大脑造的玩具。 In one line: the brain is real; the body and the world are toys we built for that brain.

你可能想问You might be wondering

它是活的吗?Is it alive?
不是。那只果蝇很多年前就不在了,留下来的是它大脑的「电线图」。我们做的事,是让这张图重新通电——有点像照着录下来的乐谱,把一首歌再演奏一遍。 No. That fly has been gone for years; what remains is the wiring diagram of its brain. What we do is put electricity back through the diagram — a bit like performing a song again from a written-down score.
它听我的话吗?Does it do what I say?
不听。你只能给它「感觉」,决定是它自己那 45,808 个细胞投票出来的。有时候你放了糖它不理你——那是真的不想理你。 No. You can only give it sensations; the decisions are voted on by its own 45,808 cells. Sometimes you put sugar down and it ignores you — it genuinely can't be bothered.
它怎么老发呆?Why does it space out?
真苍蝇也发呆呀。而且我们只偷听了七条电话线——它脑子里也许正热闹着, 只是这七条线没说话。 Real flies space out too. And we only listen to seven phone lines — its brain might be busy with something, just not on any line we can hear.
我的手机算得动吗?Can my phone really run this?
一秒要算一万步。手机要是累了,画质会自己降一档省力气—— 但大脑永远全速跑,一步都不少。 Ten thousand steps a second. If your phone gets tired, the graphics quietly drop a notch to save power — but the brain always runs at full speed, never skipping a step.
